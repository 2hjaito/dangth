2025 marks the strong return of **bold design** and **refined user experience**. Here are 8 web design trends you **shouldn't miss** if you're planning to develop a new website interface.

---

## 1. **Interactive 3D – Vivid 3D interfaces**

3D design is no longer just a supporting element. In 2025, it plays a central role in creating a **vivid experience** and boosting credibility, especially in fields like **real estate**, **architecture**, or **product showcases**.

![](/images/post/2025-07-02-top-web-design-trends-2025/3d.armanidotcom.gif)

Example: [https://3d.armani.com](https://3d.armani.com) – a website that uses a visual, rotating product effect.

---

## 2. **Lucemorphism – The glass-and-shadow style**

The **lucemorphism** style (let's just call it *loo-che-mo-ri-zim*) is a combination of **transparency** and **soft drop shadows**. The result is a design that's both **elegant** and **high-tech**.

## 4. **Bezos Scrolling – Buttery-smooth scrolling effects**

The term **"Bezos Scrolling"** refers to the kind of smooth scroll effect seen on Amazon or premium e-commerce sites – it keeps users engaged longer and creates a more professional feel.

📌 Tip: Pair it with `scroll-snap` or the `Lenis` library to enhance the experience.


![](/images/post/2025-07-02-top-web-design-trends-2025/scroll.gif)
---

## 5. **Minimalism Elevated – Minimalism with depth**

This trend emphasizes **simplicity**, **refinement**, and removing distracting elements. Soft colors with low contrast suit brands aiming for a **premium**, **elegant** feel.

Suggestion: [https://framer.com](https://framer.com) – a homepage with a minimalist yet powerful style.

---

## 6. **Oversized Typography – Big, bold fonts**

Large fonts don't just create a **strong visual impact** — they also help make **brand identity** clearer. This works great for personal portfolio sites or product landing pages.

🖼 Reference: [https://www.pitch.com](https://www.pitch.com) – uses typography as the main design element.

---

## 7. **Narrative Design – Storytelling through visuals**

Design driven by **storytelling** is making a strong comeback. Each section of the website becomes a small **chapter**, naturally guiding users through the brand's journey.

🖼 Suggestion: [https://emmanuel.design](https://emmanuel.design) – an example illustrating storytelling through visuals and animation.

---

## 8. **AI-Personalized UI – AI-personalized interfaces**

The rise of AI means website design is no longer "one size fits all." The interface can adapt based on **behavior**, **geography**, or **purchase history** — significantly boosting conversion rates.

📌 Real-world examples: Amazon, Netflix, or Shopify Stores that show recommendations tailored to each user.
